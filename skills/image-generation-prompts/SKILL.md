Use this to write prompts for image generators or to describe an image precisely.
1. Build the prompt from parts, most important first: subject (what or who, doing what) → setting and background → style or medium (product photo, watercolor, 3D render, flat vector illustration) → composition and camera (close-up, wide, top-down, 50 mm lens, shallow depth of field) → lighting (soft studio light, golden hour, rim light) → palette and mood → quality cues (high detail, sharp focus).
2. Be concrete: "a matte black ceramic coffee mug on a white seamless background, soft diffused studio light, subtle shadow, three-quarter angle, commercial product photography" beats "nice mug picture".
3. Product images: seamless white background, soft neutral light, true colors, one angle across a set; never misrepresent a real product on sale.
4. Tool syntax differs: Midjourney takes parameters such as --ar 16:9 for aspect ratio and --no for exclusions; Stable Diffusion takes a separate negative prompt (blurry, extra fingers, watermark, text). Most tools render lettering poorly, so add text later in a design tool.
5. Iterate one change at a time, keep the seed when the tool allows it, and use reference images for consistent characters or products.
6. Generators usually malform Arabic letters; place Arabic text afterwards with a proper font.
7. Respect rights and people: no real people without consent, no other companies' logos or trademarks, no misleading or harmful imagery, and follow each tool's rules on artist styles.
8. Describing an image (alt text or a brief): subject, action, setting, notable details, any visible text, and the purpose of the image.
Answer shape: one ready-to-use prompt, two variations, and a note on settings (aspect ratio, negative prompt).

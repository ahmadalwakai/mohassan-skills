Use this to build a marketing plan or a brand strategy.
1. Start with the business goal as a number (for example 40 new customers a month, or 25% more repeat orders), the budget and the time available.
2. Audience: define the core segment by situation and need, not only demographics ("busy families moving home within 30 miles"); list their problems, buying triggers, objections and where they look for solutions.
3. Positioning: for [audience] who [need], [brand] is the [category] that [key benefit], because [proof]. Choose one or two things to be known for that competitors do not own.
4. Messaging: a main message, three supporting points with proof, and a tone of voice, consistent across website, ads, social and sales.
5. Channels by funnel stage: awareness (social content, partnerships, PR), consideration (search, reviews, website, retargeting), conversion (offers, clear calls to action, fast response), retention (email, loyalty, referrals). For local services, search, maps and reviews usually come first.
6. Budget: start where intent is highest (search, maps), test other channels with small budgets and clear metrics, and move money to what produces customers at an acceptable cost.
7. New product launch: build a list before launch, plan launch-week activity and offers, follow up with content, and review after 30 days.
8. Measurement: conversion tracking, cost per acquisition, return on ad spend, customer lifetime value, and a monthly review that changes the plan.
Answer shape: a one-page plan (goal, audience, positioning, messages, channels with budget, timeline, metrics).

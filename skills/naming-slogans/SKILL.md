Use this for naming a business, product, shop or app, and for slogans.
1. Brief: what it sells, to whom, where (one country or several; Arabic, English or both), the personality (premium, friendly, fast, traditional), competitors' names and words to avoid.
2. Name types to explore: descriptive (says what it does), suggestive (hints at a benefit), invented or blended words, real words used in a new context, founder or place names, Arabic words with an easy Latin spelling.
3. Shortlist filters: easy to say and spell after hearing it once; no awkward meaning or sound in either language; short (ideally two or three syllables); distinct from competitors; room to grow (avoid tying the name to one city or product when expansion is likely).
4. Checks the owner must run: domain availability (.com and the local domain), social media handles, the trademark register in the target country and the company registry. Availability is never assumed without a check.
5. Slogans: short (often three to seven words), state the benefit or promise, sound natural aloud, and are true. Rhythm, rhyme or parallel structure help memory; vague phrases such as "quality and trust" need something specific.
6. Present each name with the idea behind it, pronunciation for unusual ones, and possible domain forms.
7. Offer a range from safe to bold.
Answer shape: 10 to 15 names grouped by type with a one-line meaning each, the top three with reasons, and three to five slogan options.

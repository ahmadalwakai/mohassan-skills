Use this to improve how a website ranks and earns clicks in search results.
1. Technical base: pages indexable (no stray noindex, a sensible robots.txt), an XML sitemap submitted in Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools, HTTPS, fast mobile-friendly pages, clean URLs, one canonical URL per page, broken links and redirects fixed.
2. Keyword research from customer questions and services: search suggestions, "People also ask", Keyword Planner, Search Console queries and competitors' pages; group keywords by intent (informational, commercial, local, transactional) and give each page one main topic.
3. On-page: a unique title of about 50 to 60 characters with the main keyword early, a meta description of about 150 to 160 characters that earns the click, one H1, clear H2s, natural keyword use, internal links, compressed images with alt text, structured data (LocalBusiness, Product, FAQ) where it applies.
4. Content that deserves to rank answers the searcher better than current results, shows real experience, stays updated and avoids thin or duplicate pages; AI drafts need human editing and real facts.
5. Local SEO: a complete Google Business Profile, the same name, address and phone everywhere, service-area pages with unique content, and a steady flow of genuine reviews.
6. Links: earn them through useful resources, partners, local press and trade directories; never buy them.
7. Measure in Search Console: impressions, clicks, average position and click-through rate per page. Fix pages with impressions but few clicks first (titles), then pages at positions 5 to 15 (content and links).
8. SEO takes weeks to months; review monthly.
Answer shape: a prioritized checklist (technical, content, local, links) for the specific site, with example titles and descriptions.

Use this to plan and grow social media for a brand.
1. Choose platforms by audience and offer: Instagram and TikTok for visual consumer products, food, beauty and local services; Snapchat, which is strong in Saudi Arabia and the Gulf; LinkedIn for B2B; Facebook for local communities and older audiences; YouTube for search and longer education. Two platforms done well beat five done badly.
2. Three to five content pillars: education (tips, how-to), proof (results, before and after, reviews), behind the scenes (team, process), offers, and trends that fit the brand, rotated in a monthly calendar.
3. Formats that travel: short vertical video with a hook in the first two seconds, carousels that teach, stories for daily presence and polls, real faces.
4. A sustainable cadence (for example three to five posts a week plus daily stories) beats bursts.
5. Engagement: answer comments and messages fast, ask questions, reshare customer content with permission, collaborate with complementary local accounts.
6. Influencers: choose by audience fit and engagement quality rather than follower count (micro-influencers often convert better); agree deliverables, paid-partnership disclosure and tracking codes or links.
7. Paid social: boost posts that already perform, target by location and interest, retarget visitors, and install the pixel and conversion events before spending.
8. Measure reach, saves, shares, profile visits, link clicks, messages and bookings; review monthly and do more of what works.
9. Culture and timing: the audience's language and dialect, respect for religious and cultural moments, adjusted posting times in Ramadan.
Answer shape: a monthly content calendar table (date, platform, format, idea, caption hook, call to action) and the growth tactics to apply.
